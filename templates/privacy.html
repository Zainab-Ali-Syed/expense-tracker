Privacy Policy

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, including your email address for account creation and any financial transactions you choose to log. We also collect basic usage data to improve the app experience.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. This includes calculating your spending totals, categorizing expenses, and providing you with financial summaries.

3. Data Storage

We employ industry-standard security measures to protect your data. Your financial records are stored securely and are accessible only to you through your authenticated account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We do not share your financial data with any external advertising or data brokerage services.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact our support team at privacy@spendly.example.com.
